# Dream World player guide

Open Minigame practice to play any game without a sleeping Pokémon. During a regular visit, the How to play link above the game opens in a separate tab.

[Illustrated Dream World guide](http://127.0.0.1:8080/player-guide/pictures/)

## From tuck-in to the Entree Forest

1. On the C-Gear touch screen, choose Wi-Fi → Game Sync and tuck in a Pokémon. Wait for the transfer to finish, then open the Dream World with the same game selected on the website.
2. From your house, follow the arrows to the rainbow bridge and cross to the Island of Dreams. Click moving grass, icicles or other shaking objects to look for Pokémon. Click a sparkle to collect an item.
3. Play a minigame with a Pokémon you meet. At the Tree of Dreams, click the hollow, offer a Berry and choose one of the Pokémon you befriended.
4. At the house, use the chest to choose items to take back. Select a C-Gear skin under Customize if you want one. Use the power button at the top right of the Dream World and confirm you are ready to wake up.
5. Open Wi-Fi → Game Sync on the same save and wake your sleeping Pokémon. When the transfer finishes, choose Wireless → Entralink, walk north into the Entree Forest and talk to your new partner to catch it.

## Send Friendly Mail

Open the friend list on your profile and choose the mail button beside a trade partner or Dream Pal. The compose page selects that recipient. Choose a preset message, send it, and open Sent to check it. Your friend can read it in their inbox when they sign in with the corresponding game on the same server. Both players can use mail while their Pokémon are awake.

## Minigame controls

### Pokémon Seek

- Remember the missing Pokémon’s name. Follow the arrows to change areas and click shaking grass, trees or rocks to look inside.
- Use Listen to hear its cry. A louder cry means you are closer. Pick up the sparkling lost item and search nearby.

### Ice Cream Scoop

- Choose a Berry. Hold the mouse button over an ice cream tub to grow a scoop, then drag it over the dish and release.
- Aim for the sample size. Mix the three flavours and start near the middle so the tower stays upright.

### Sky Race

- Move the flag with the mouse. Your Pokémon follows it. Reach the finish before time runs out.
- Avoid clouds and weakness symbols. Collect three matching type symbols to boost your speed. Press Space to pause.

### Wailord’s Water Spout

- Move Wailord beneath a falling Pokémon. Click to send up a waterspout and bounce it back into the air.
- Aim at a balloon showing that Pokémon. Keep watching for the next Pokémon to fall and chain hits for a bonus.

### Pokémon Café · Serve Drinks

- Read each customer’s order in its bubble. Click to pick up a drink and move the bell to guide Foongus with the tray.
- Click in front of the customer who ordered that drink. Move smoothly so that drinks stay on the tray.

### Victini’s Treasure Box

- Guide Victini with the light. Avoid rocks and collect type energy. A macaron gives a temporary power-up.
- At the chest, use the stored energy with Reshiram or Zekrom. After completing the regular promotion, check Customize for its C-Gear skin.

### Blow Out Candles

- Click one or more lit candles in an unbroken horizontal row, then confirm to blow them out.
- The player who puts out the last flame loses. Try to leave the last one for your opponent.

### Gem Collector

- At a fork, hold the mouse button and trace the symbol above your chosen track in one stroke. Release to choose it.
- Look ahead for gems. Hitting an obstacle can knock gems out of your cart.

### Frozen Treat Sweep

- Click a patch of ice to skate towards it. Watch where sweets land and collect them before your opponents.
- Change direction as the ice cracks. Falling in the water costs time.

### Skip Across the Lake

- Choose a board and rider. Click when the power gauge is near full, then click to set the angle.
- Time your jump and move the mouse in the air to tilt the board. Use the wind and trampolines as you aim for 1,500 m.

### Pokémon Bistro · Serve Drinks

- Check the colours of the orders. Click the counter for a drink and guide Foongus to the waiting customer.
- Move gently. Click to hand over the right cup and complete as many orders as possible before time runs out.

### Charizard’s Treasure Box

- Move the light to guide Charizard. Collect type energy and steer around rocks.
- Use a power-up to break rocks. At the treasure chest, use the energy you have collected.
